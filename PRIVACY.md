# Privacy Policy for ExotiTrack

**Last updated: November 25, 2026**

ExotiTrack ("the app," "we," "us") is an Android application for tracking the care of exotic animals. This policy explains what the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. We keep it simple: the app is designed to work offline, and cloud features are optional and only active when you choose to use them.

## The short version

- You can use ExotiTrack **fully offline with no account**. In that mode, your data stays on your device and we receive nothing.
- If you **sign in** (to back up, sync across devices, or share), your data is stored in Google Firebase and tied to your account.
- We **do not sell your data**, and we do not use it for advertising.

## What we collect

**When you use the app offline (no sign-in):**
- The information you enter, animal profiles, log entries (feeding, weight, sheds, health, maintenance, measurements), notes, reminders, and any photos you add. This is stored **only on your device**.

**When you sign in (optional):**
- **Account information** from Google Sign-In, your email address and basic profile identifier, used to identify your account. We do not receive your Google password.
- **Your tracking data and photos**, synced to our cloud (Google Firebase) so you can back up, use multiple devices, and share.
- **Sharing information**, if you invite someone to your collection or join someone else's, the membership (who has access and their role) is stored so the feature can work.

We do **not** collect your location, contacts, or device identifiers for advertising, and the app does not include third-party analytics or ad networks at this time.

## How your information is used

- To provide the app's core features, storing and displaying your records.
- To back up and sync your data across your own devices, when signed in.
- To enable sharing with people you explicitly invite.
- To send reminder notifications you have configured (these are generated on your device).

We do not use your data to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

## Where your data is stored and processed

The app works locally on your device. When you sign in, cloud data is stored and processed by **Google Firebase** (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Storage), acting as our data processor. Data in transit is encrypted, and cloud data is tied to your authenticated account. Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's own privacy and security terms.

## Sharing with other people

Sharing is entirely under your control. If you invite someone into your collection, the animals and records in that collection become visible to them according to the role you grant (viewer, editor, or owner). If you join someone else's collection, they control what you can see. You can remove members or leave a collection at any time.

## Your choices and control

- **Stay local:** don't sign in, and nothing leaves your device.
- **Export:** use "Back up to file" to save a copy of your data at any time.
- **Delete:** remove individual records or animals in the app. To delete your cloud data, you can remove your records while signed in, or contact us (below) to request deletion of your account data.
- **Stop syncing:** sign out to stop cloud sync on a device.

## Data retention

Local data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Cloud data is retained while your account is active and is removed when you delete it or request account deletion.

## Children

ExotiTrack is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will remove it.

## Changes to this policy

If features change (for example, if advertising or new cloud options are added), we will update this policy and revise the "Last updated" date above.
