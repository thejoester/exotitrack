# ExotiTrack

**Exotic animal husbandry tracker for Android.**

ExotiTrack helps keepers record and care for exotic pets, reptiles, amphibians, invertebrates, and more. Keep a profile for every animal, log feedings, weights, sheds, health and maintenance, get reminders, chart growth, and (optionally) sync and share your collection with family. The app works fully offline; cloud features are opt-in.

## Features

- **Animal profiles**, species, morph, sex, key dates, a photo, and a custom card color.
- **Logging**, feeding, weight, shed, health, cleaning/maintenance, measurements, and notes, per animal.
- **Customizable categories and sub-groups** so the app fits what you actually keep.
- **Quick actions**, one-tap logging from a swipe or a grid tile, configurable per animal.
- **Reminders** (feeding, weighing, and custom) with notifications.
- **Charts** for weight and measurements over time.
- **Per-entry photos**.
- **Dashboard** with totals and a "needs feeding" view.
- **Search, sort, and filter**, with list or grid (tile) layouts.
- **Archive** animals you no longer actively track.
- **Backup and restore** your whole collection to a file.
- **Cloud sync** across your own devices (optional, requires sign-in).
- **Sharing**, invite family or a co-keeper into your collection with owner, editor, or viewer roles, via an invite code or email.

## Planned

- **Breeding / gestation module**, pairings, clutches/incubation, projected hatch/birth, and offspring lineage.
- **QR enclosure labels**, print a code, scan to jump to the animal.
- **CSV / PDF export**, e.g. one animal's history for a vet visit.
- **Environment tracking**, temperature and humidity per enclosure.
- **Localization**, starting with Spanish.
- **Auto-backup to your own Google Drive or Dropbox**.
- **Subscription tier** for cloud backup, multi-device sync, photo sync, and larger sharing groups (basic solo use and sharing with one person stay free).
